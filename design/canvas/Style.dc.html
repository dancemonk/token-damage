VOICE · THE ADJUSTER
Numbers do the talking.
“+95% in 3 months” beats “RAM prices are insane”. If a line needs an adjective, cut the adjective.
Mock the situation, never the person.
The adjuster is tired, not cruel. He has seen everything and is still surprised by you.
Every number wears its label.
Plain = measured. ≡ = list price. ≈ = estimate, always a range. ✶ = satire, always red. Never mix them.
